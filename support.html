01Getting started

How do I use LyricCam at a concert?
  1. Open LyricCam and tap Record now.
  2. Type the song in the What song? box and pick it. Or tap Detect song, to the left of the record button, while the music is playing.
  3. The lyrics appear over the camera. Press the red button to record.
  4. Press it again to stop. The video is saved straight to Photos.

Venues often have a weak signal. If you know what will be played, prepare a setlist at home first. See Setlists.

Are the lyrics recorded into my video?

No. Lyrics are shown on your screen only. The saved video does not contain the lyrics.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The app records with the back camera and works in portrait. There is no iPad or Android version on the App Store.

How do I change the app language?

LyricCam is available in English and Bahasa Melayu. In the app, go to Settings > Language. This opens iPhone Settings for LyricCam, where you can choose the language.

02Lyrics

The lyrics are wrong, or are for a different version of the song.

Tap the song name under the lyrics, then Wrong lyrics?. Pick another version from the list. LyricCam remembers your choice for that song.

Lyrics come from LRCLIB, a community lyrics database. Sometimes no correct version exists yet. If nothing in the list is right, tap Search again and try a different spelling or the original title.

The lyrics are out of sync with the music.
  • Sync by ear. Tap Sync lyrics, to the left of the record button, while the music is playing. The app listens for a few seconds and jumps to the right line. This needs an internet connection.
  • Sync by hand. Drag the lyrics up or down to the line being sung. Tap the lyrics to pause or resume them.
  • Auto sync. Press and hold Sync lyrics and turn on Auto sync, or use Settings > Auto sync. The app then checks again about every 40 seconds.

Sync is switched off while you are recording, so your video keeps clean sound. Sync first, then record. While recording you can still drag the lyrics.

Live performances are often faster, slower or arranged differently from the studio recording, so some drift at a concert is normal. A few songs only have plain lyrics with no timing. The app tells you, and you scroll those yourself.

Detect song did not recognise the song.
  • Move closer to the speakers and try again.
  • Check your internet connection. Detection needs one.
  • Detection works best with the original recording. Live versions, covers and remixes may not match.

You can always type the song name in What song? instead.

The app says there are no lyrics for my song.

Not every song is in the LRCLIB database, and instrumental tracks have no lyrics. Try another spelling, the original title, or a different release of the same song.

Can I read Korean, Japanese or Chinese lyrics in Latin letters?

Yes. Tap the song name under the lyrics and use the script switch to change between the original script and romanised lyrics. To make romanised lyrics the default, turn on Settings > Romanised lyrics first.

Can I change how the lyrics look?

Yes. Open Lyric style from Home, from Settings, or by tapping the song name in the camera. You can change the text size, font, colour of the current line, background and number of lines (2 to 4).

03Recording and videos

Where are my videos?

In the Photos app, like any other video you record. In the camera, the small thumbnail at the top right is your latest clip. Tap it to replay or share.

My video was not saved.

LyricCam needs permission to add videos to Photos. Open iPhone Settings > LyricCam > Photos and allow adding photos. Then go back to the app and try saving again from the message on screen.

Also check that your iPhone has free storage. 4K video uses a lot of space. Recording settings shows roughly how many minutes you have left.

My video has no sound.

Microphone access is off. Open iPhone Settings > LyricCam and turn on Microphone.

How do I change video quality?

In the camera, tap the small format label under the lens buttons, for example "4K · 30 FPS". Or open Recording settings from Home. You can choose resolution, frame rate, video format, stabilisation and stereo or mono sound. The options depend on your iPhone model. Changes apply when you are not recording.

Can I use the front camera?

Not in this version. LyricCam records with the back camera.

04Setlists

How do I prepare songs before a concert?
  1. Open a song in the camera, tap the song name, then Add to setlist.
  2. Create a setlist and add the rest of your songs the same way.
  3. Open the setlist and tap Download all while you have a good connection. The lyrics are stored on your iPhone.

At the venue, open a song from the setlist and use the arrows beside the song name to move to the previous or next song. Making your own setlists is free.

What are prepared concert setlists?

Home lists upcoming concerts. Open one to see the setlist the artist is expected to play. With LyricCam Premium, tap Take this setlist. The app loads the lyrics for the whole setlist and opens the camera on the first song.

Expected setlists are estimates gathered from public sources. Artists often change the songs or the order on the night. Songs with no lyrics available are skipped.

05Premium subscription

What do I get with LyricCam Premium?

In version 1.0, Premium lets you take prepared concert setlists in one tap. Everything else in the app is free. In Malaysia, Premium costs RM19.90 a month or RM99 a year. The price in your country is shown in the app before you confirm.

How do I cancel my subscription?
  1. Open iPhone Settings and tap your name at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Choose LyricCam, then tap Cancel Subscription.

You can also open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Premium stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose to request a refund for the LyricCam purchase. We cannot issue refunds ourselves because we never receive your payment details.

I subscribed, but Premium is not active. Or I have a new iPhone.

Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe. Then open LyricCam Settings and tap Restore purchases. If it still does not work, email us.

06Permissions and privacy

Which permissions does LyricCam need, and why?
  • Camera. To show the camera view and record video.
  • Microphone. To record sound in your videos, and to detect the song playing nearby so the lyrics stay in time.
  • Photos, add only. To save your videos. LyricCam cannot see your photo library.

To change them, open iPhone Settings > LyricCam. Or in the app, go to Settings > Camera, microphone and Photos access.

Is LyricCam listening all the time?

No. Apart from recording your videos, the app listens only when you tap Detect song or Sync lyrics, for about 12 seconds. If you turn on Auto sync, it also checks again every 40 seconds or so while the camera is open. It never listens in the background. iOS shows an orange dot whenever the microphone is in use.

Do you upload or see my videos?

No. Videos are saved to your own Photos library and never sent to us. There is no account and no tracking. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

01Mula guna

Macam mana nak guna LyricCam di konsert?
  1. Buka LyricCam dan tekan Rakam sekarang.
  2. Taip nama lagu dalam kotak Lagu apa? dan pilih. Atau tekan Kesan lagu, di kiri butang rakam, semasa muzik dimainkan.
  3. Lirik muncul di atas kamera. Tekan butang merah untuk merakam.
  4. Tekan sekali lagi untuk berhenti. Video terus disimpan ke Photos.

Talian di venue selalunya lemah. Jika anda tahu lagu yang akan dimainkan, sediakan setlist di rumah dahulu. Lihat Setlist.

Adakah lirik dirakam ke dalam video saya?

Tidak. Lirik dipaparkan di skrin anda sahaja. Video yang disimpan tidak mengandungi lirik.

Peranti apa yang disokong?

iPhone dengan iOS 17 atau lebih baharu. App merakam dengan kamera belakang dalam orientasi potret. Tiada versi iPad atau Android di App Store.

Macam mana nak tukar bahasa app?

LyricCam tersedia dalam bahasa Inggeris dan Bahasa Melayu. Dalam app, pergi ke Tetapan > Bahasa. Ini membuka Tetapan iPhone untuk LyricCam, dan di situ anda boleh memilih bahasa.

02Lirik

Lirik salah, atau lirik untuk versi lagu yang lain.

Tekan nama lagu di bawah lirik, kemudian Lirik salah?. Pilih versi lain daripada senarai. LyricCam akan ingat pilihan anda untuk lagu itu.

Lirik datang daripada LRCLIB, pangkalan data lirik komuniti. Kadang-kadang versi yang betul memang belum ada. Jika tiada yang betul dalam senarai, tekan Cari semula dan cuba ejaan lain atau tajuk asal.

Lirik tidak selaras dengan muzik.
  • Selaras ikut bunyi. Tekan Sync lirik, di kiri butang rakam, semasa muzik dimainkan. App mendengar beberapa saat dan melompat ke baris yang betul. Ini memerlukan internet.
  • Selaras sendiri. Seret lirik ke atas atau ke bawah sehingga sampai baris yang sedang dinyanyikan. Ketik lirik untuk jeda atau sambung.
  • Auto sync. Tekan lama Sync lirik dan hidupkan Auto sync, atau guna Tetapan > Auto sync. App akan menyemak semula lebih kurang setiap 40 saat.

Sync dimatikan semasa anda merakam supaya bunyi video kekal bersih. Sync dahulu, kemudian rakam. Semasa merakam, anda masih boleh menyeret lirik.

Persembahan langsung selalunya lebih laju, lebih perlahan atau disusun berbeza daripada rakaman studio, jadi sedikit lari di konsert itu biasa. Ada lagu yang hanya ada lirik biasa tanpa timing. App akan memaklumkan, dan anda perlu menyeretnya sendiri.

Kesan lagu tak dapat kenal lagu.
  • Pergi lebih dekat dengan pembesar suara dan cuba lagi.
  • Semak sambungan internet. Kesan lagu memerlukannya.
  • Kesan lagu paling berkesan dengan rakaman asal. Versi live, cover dan remix mungkin tidak sepadan.

Anda sentiasa boleh menaip nama lagu dalam Lagu apa?.

App kata tiada lirik untuk lagu saya.

Tidak semua lagu ada dalam pangkalan data LRCLIB, dan lagu instrumental memang tiada lirik. Cuba ejaan lain, tajuk asal, atau keluaran lain untuk lagu yang sama.

Boleh baca lirik Korea, Jepun atau Cina dalam huruf rumi?

Boleh. Tekan nama lagu di bawah lirik dan guna suis tulisan untuk tukar antara tulisan asal dan lirik rumi. Untuk jadikan rumi sebagai lalai, hidupkan Tetapan > Lirik rumi dulu.

Boleh tukar rupa lirik?

Boleh. Buka Gaya lirik dari Home, dari Tetapan, atau dengan menekan nama lagu di kamera. Anda boleh tukar saiz teks, fon, warna baris semasa, latar dan bilangan baris (2 hingga 4).

03Rakaman dan video

Di mana video saya?

Dalam app Photos, sama seperti video lain yang anda rakam. Di kamera, lakaran kecil di kanan atas ialah klip terakhir anda. Tekan untuk main semula atau kongsi.

Video saya tak tersimpan.

LyricCam perlukan kebenaran untuk menambah video ke Photos. Buka Tetapan iPhone > LyricCam > Photos dan benarkan tambah foto. Kemudian kembali ke app dan cuba simpan semula daripada mesej di skrin.

Semak juga storan kosong iPhone anda. Video 4K menggunakan banyak ruang. Tetapan rakaman memaparkan anggaran baki minit rakaman.

Video saya tiada bunyi.

Akses mikrofon dimatikan. Buka Tetapan iPhone > LyricCam dan hidupkan Mikrofon.

Macam mana nak tukar kualiti video?

Di kamera, tekan label format kecil di bawah butang lensa, contohnya "4K · 30 FPS". Atau buka Tetapan rakaman dari Home. Anda boleh pilih resolusi, kadar bingkai, format video, penstabilan dan bunyi stereo atau mono. Pilihan bergantung pada model iPhone anda. Perubahan berkuat kuasa apabila anda tidak sedang merakam.

Boleh guna kamera depan?

Belum dalam versi ini. LyricCam merakam dengan kamera belakang.

04Setlist

Macam mana nak sediakan lagu sebelum konsert?
  1. Buka lagu di kamera, tekan nama lagu, kemudian Tambah ke setlist.
  2. Cipta setlist dan tambah lagu lain dengan cara yang sama.
  3. Buka setlist itu dan tekan Muat turun semua semasa sambungan internet baik. Lirik disimpan dalam iPhone anda.

Di venue, buka lagu daripada setlist dan guna anak panah di sebelah nama lagu untuk ke lagu sebelum atau seterusnya. Membuat setlist sendiri adalah percuma.

Apa itu setlist konsert yang disediakan?

Home menyenaraikan konsert akan datang. Buka satu konsert untuk melihat setlist yang dijangka dipersembahkan. Dengan LyricCam Premium, tekan Ambil setlist ini. App memuatkan lirik untuk seluruh setlist dan membuka kamera pada lagu pertama.

Setlist jangkaan ialah anggaran yang dikumpul daripada sumber awam. Artis sering menukar lagu atau susunan pada malam persembahan. Lagu yang tiada lirik dilangkau.

06Kebenaran dan privasi

Kebenaran apa yang LyricCam perlukan, dan kenapa?
  • Kamera. Untuk memaparkan pandangan kamera dan merakam video.
  • Mikrofon. Untuk merakam bunyi dalam video, dan mengesan lagu yang sedang dimainkan berdekatan supaya lirik kekal selaras.
  • Photos, tambah sahaja. Untuk menyimpan video anda. LyricCam tidak boleh melihat galeri anda.

Untuk menukarnya, buka Tetapan iPhone > LyricCam. Atau dalam app, pergi ke Tetapan > Akses kamera, mikrofon dan Photos.

Adakah LyricCam mendengar sepanjang masa?

Tidak. Selain merakam video anda, app hanya mendengar apabila anda menekan Kesan lagu atau Sync lirik, selama kira-kira 12 saat. Jika anda menghidupkan Auto sync, app juga menyemak semula lebih kurang setiap 40 saat semasa kamera dibuka. App tidak pernah mendengar di latar belakang. iOS memaparkan titik oren setiap kali mikrofon digunakan.

Adakah anda memuat naik atau melihat video saya?

Tidak. Video disimpan ke Photos anda sendiri dan tidak pernah dihantar kepada kami. Tiada akaun dan tiada penjejakan. Butiran penuh ada dalam Dasar Privasi.
