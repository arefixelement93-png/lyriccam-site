How it worksFour things it does well

  • 01 · Search

    Find the song

    Type a title or an artist. LyricCam finds the song and loads its lyrics, with timing when it is available.

  • 02 · Detect

    Or let it listen

    Tap Detect song while the music plays. The app recognises the song and jumps to the line being sung.

  • 03 · Record

    Film in full quality

    Up to 4K at 60 fps where your iPhone supports it, with stereo sound. Videos save straight to Photos.

  • 04 · Setlist

    Plan the whole night

    Build a setlist, download the lyrics before you leave home, and step through the songs at the venue, even with no signal.

PremiumLyricCam Premium

Home lists upcoming concerts with the setlist each artist is expected to play. With Premium, take a prepared setlist in one tap: the lyrics for the whole show are loaded and the camera opens on the first song.

Good to knowStraight answers

  • Lyrics appear on your screen only. They are not added to the video.
  • Lyrics come from LRCLIB, a community database. Some songs are missing, and timing can drift, especially with live versions. You can pick another version or drag the lyrics yourself.
  • Expected setlists are estimates. Artists change them.
  • Check the venue's rules before you record.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy.

Kamera konsert untuk iPhone

Lirik di skrin.
Bukan dalam video.

LyricCam memaparkan lirik lagu semasa anda merakam konsert, supaya anda boleh menyanyi sama dan terus merakam. Lirik kekal di skrin anda dan tidak masuk ke dalam video.

Untuk iPhone dengan iOS 17 atau lebih baharu. Percuma untuk dimuat turun, dengan langganan Premium pilihan.

Cara ia berfungsiEmpat perkara yang ia buat dengan baik

  • 01 · Cari

    Cari lagu

    Taip tajuk atau nama artis. LyricCam mencari lagu itu dan memuatkan liriknya, lengkap dengan timing jika ada.

  • 02 · Kesan

    Atau biar ia dengar

    Tekan Kesan lagu semasa muzik dimainkan. App mengenal lagu itu dan melompat ke baris yang sedang dinyanyikan.

  • 03 · Rakam

    Rakam kualiti penuh

    Sehingga 4K pada 60 fps jika iPhone anda menyokongnya, dengan bunyi stereo. Video terus disimpan ke Photos.

  • 04 · Setlist

    Sedia untuk satu malam

    Bina setlist, muat turun lirik sebelum keluar rumah, dan tukar lagu satu demi satu di venue, walaupun tiada talian.
