Privacy Policy

LyricCam is a concert camera for iPhone. It shows the lyrics of a song on screen while you record. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

LyricCam is built and run by an independent developer in Malaysia. In this policy, "LyricCam", "we" and "us" mean that developer.

In short
  • No account. You never sign up, log in or give us your name.
  • No ads, no analytics tools, no tracking in the app.
  • Your videos go to your own Photos library. We never receive them.
  • To find a song and its lyrics, the app sends the song you look for to Apple and to LRCLIB. They also see your IP address, as any website does.
  • Detecting a song uses Apple's ShazamKit. It works from a sound fingerprint, not from your recording.
  • Subscriptions are paid through Apple. We never see your card details.

01What stays on your iPhone

Most of what LyricCam does happens on your phone and goes nowhere else.

  • Your videos and their sound. A video is recorded to a temporary file inside the app and then moved into your Photos library. LyricCam can only add to Photos. It cannot look at, read or delete anything in your library. So that you can replay or share the clip you just took, the app keeps one copy of your latest clip in its temporary folder. That copy is removed when you record the next clip, and anything left over is cleared the next time the app starts. The app never uploads a video. If you tap Share, iOS shows its own share sheet and the video goes only where you choose to send it.
  • Lyrics on screen. Lyrics are drawn over the camera view only. They are never recorded into your video.
  • Your settings. Lyric style, recording settings, Auto sync, your romanised lyrics preference and the country you picked for the concert list.
  • Songs and setlists. The last 12 songs you opened, with their lyrics, so they still open on a weak signal. The lyric version you chose under "Wrong lyrics?". Your setlists, with their downloaded lyrics and album covers.
  • Small caches. Album cover images and timing numbers for song previews, so the app has less to load next time.
  • The concert list. The list of upcoming concerts is built into the app and arrives with app updates. The app does not contact us to load it. Your iPhone's region setting is used on the phone to decide which country's concerts to show first.

All of this lives in the app's own storage on your iPhone. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, Apple may include it in that backup. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also clear recent songs and delete setlists inside the app.

02What leaves your iPhone, and who receives it

LyricCam has no server of its own. When the app needs something from the internet, it asks the services below directly. We cannot see these requests and we keep no copy of them.

ServiceWhenWhat is sent
Apple, iTunes Search When you type in the song search, and right after a song is detected. The text you typed, or the Apple Music ID of the detected song. Also a two-letter country code taken from your iPhone's region setting, so results match your local music catalogue.
LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a community lyrics database that we do not run When search results appear, and when you open a song, download a setlist or tap "Wrong lyrics?". The song's title, artist, album and length. If Apple's search finds nothing, your search text is sent to LRCLIB instead. The app identifies itself as "LyricCam" with a version number.
Apple, ShazamKit When you tap Detect song or Sync lyrics, while Auto sync is on, and the first time you preview a song. A short audio fingerprint that your iPhone works out from a few seconds of sound. Apple states that the audio itself is not shared with Apple and that the fingerprint cannot be turned back into audio.
Apple, album covers and previews When album covers are shown, and when you play a 30-second preview. A request for that image or clip. Previews are streamed and are not saved.
Apple, App Store When you subscribe, restore a purchase, or the app checks your subscription. The purchase is handled by Apple with your Apple Account. The app only learns whether Premium is active.

Every internet request reveals your IP address and a few basic technical details, such as the app version and iOS networking version, to the service that receives it. LyricCam does not add your name, an account, an advertising identifier, a device identifier or your GPS location to any request. Search and lyrics requests are sent with cookies switched off.

What each service does with a request is covered by its own policy. For Apple, see the Apple Privacy Policy. LRCLIB is an independent, free community service. We do not control how it handles or logs requests.

About the microphone

The microphone is used for two things only: recording the sound of your videos, and hearing the music around you so the app can detect the song and keep the lyrics in time. For detection the app listens for about 12 seconds at a time. Sound used for detection is not saved.

Auto sync is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, the app listens again every 40 seconds or so, only while the camera screen is open with a song loaded, and never while you are recording. When you preview a song, the app first matches the preview clip itself. If that does not work and you have already allowed the microphone, it listens for up to 10 seconds instead. iOS shows its orange dot whenever the microphone is in use.

Links that leave the app

"Listen on Apple Music" and the source links on concert pages open in another app or in your browser. Those sites and apps have their own privacy policies.

03What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account.
  • No videos, photos or audio recordings. They never leave your phone through LyricCam.
  • No contacts and no location. The app never asks for either.
  • No advertising identifier and no device identifier.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising code from other companies.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no selling of data.

04Permissions LyricCam asks for

PermissionWhyIf you say no
Camera To show the camera view and record your videos. You cannot record. Song search and lyrics still work.
Microphone To record sound in your videos, and to detect the song playing nearby so the lyrics stay in time. Videos are recorded without sound, and song detection and sync are not available.
Photos, add only To save the videos you record to your Photos library. The video cannot be saved. The app tells you and lets you try again.

The app asks for the camera and microphone the first time you open the camera, and for Photos the first time a video is saved. You can change any of these at any time in iPhone Settings > LyricCam. LyricCam does not ask for your location, contacts, notifications or permission to track you.

05Subscriptions and payments

LyricCam Premium is an optional subscription sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple takes the payment using your Apple Account (Apple ID). We never receive your card number, billing address or Apple Account email.

On your iPhone, Apple tells the app whether a Premium subscription is active so the app can unlock Premium. Apple also gives us sales reports with totals. Those reports do not tell us who you are.

06Diagnostics from Apple

LyricCam contains no crash reporting or analytics code. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers (iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may show us crash reports and usage totals that do not identify you. Apple collects these, not LyricCam.

07If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write or attach. We use it only to answer you and to fix the problem. We keep the conversation for as long as it is useful for support, and we delete it if you ask.

08Children

LyricCam is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

09Keeping, deleting and your choices

  • App data on your iPhone stays there until you clear it or delete the app.
  • You can switch off any permission in iPhone Settings, and switch off Auto sync in the app's Settings.
  • We hold no account or profile for you, so there is nothing for us to look up or delete, apart from emails you have sent us.
  • Requests to Apple and LRCLIB may be handled in other countries.

Depending on where you live, laws such as Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act, or the GDPR in the EU and UK, may give you rights over your personal data, such as asking to see it, correct it or have it deleted. You can use those rights by emailing us. For data held by Apple or LRCLIB, please contact them.

10This website

These pages use no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. The company that hosts the site may keep standard access logs, such as IP address and time of visit, for security.

11Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if we add accounts, analytics or a server of our own, we will update this page first, change the date at the top, and mention it in the app's update notes.

Dasar Privasi

LyricCam ialah kamera konsert untuk iPhone. App ini memaparkan lirik lagu di skrin semasa anda merakam. Halaman ini menerangkan, dalam bahasa mudah, apa yang app buat dengan maklumat anda.

LyricCam dibina dan dikendalikan oleh seorang pembangun bebas di Malaysia. Dalam dasar ini, "LyricCam" dan "kami" bermaksud pembangun tersebut. Jika versi Bahasa Melayu dan versi Inggeris berbeza maksud, versi Inggeris diguna pakai.

Ringkasnya
  • Tiada akaun. Anda tidak perlu mendaftar, log masuk atau beri nama.
  • Tiada iklan, tiada alat analitik, tiada penjejakan dalam app.
  • Video anda masuk ke Photos anda sendiri. Kami tidak pernah menerimanya.
  • Untuk mencari lagu dan liriknya, app menghantar lagu yang anda cari kepada Apple dan LRCLIB. Mereka juga nampak alamat IP anda, sama seperti mana-mana laman web.
  • Kesan lagu menggunakan ShazamKit Apple. Ia bekerja dengan cap jari bunyi, bukan rakaman anda.
  • Langganan dibayar melalui Apple. Kami tidak pernah nampak butiran kad anda.

01Apa yang kekal dalam iPhone anda

Kebanyakan kerja LyricCam berlaku dalam telefon anda dan tidak pergi ke mana-mana.

  • Video dan bunyinya. Video dirakam ke fail sementara dalam app, kemudian dipindahkan ke Photos anda. LyricCam hanya boleh menambah ke Photos. App tidak boleh melihat, membaca atau memadam apa-apa dalam galeri anda. Supaya anda boleh main semula atau kongsi klip yang baru dirakam, app menyimpan satu salinan klip terakhir dalam folder sementaranya. Salinan itu dibuang apabila anda merakam klip seterusnya, dan apa-apa baki dikosongkan apabila app dibuka semula. App tidak pernah memuat naik video. Jika anda tekan Kongsi, iOS memaparkan helaian kongsinya sendiri dan video hanya pergi ke tempat yang anda pilih.
  • Lirik di skrin. Lirik dilukis di atas paparan kamera sahaja. Lirik tidak pernah dirakam ke dalam video.
  • Tetapan anda. Gaya lirik, tetapan rakaman, Auto sync, pilihan lirik rumi dan negara yang anda pilih untuk senarai konsert.
  • Lagu dan setlist. 12 lagu terakhir yang anda buka, bersama liriknya, supaya masih boleh dibuka bila talian lemah. Versi lirik yang anda pilih di "Lirik salah?". Setlist anda, bersama lirik dan kulit album yang dimuat turun.
  • Cache kecil. Imej kulit album dan nombor masa untuk pratonton lagu, supaya app kurang memuat pada kali seterusnya.
  • Senarai konsert. Senarai konsert akan datang dibina dalam app dan sampai bersama kemas kini app. App tidak menghubungi kami untuk memuatkannya. Tetapan rantau iPhone anda digunakan dalam telefon untuk menentukan konsert negara mana dipaparkan dahulu.

Semua ini disimpan dalam storan app sendiri di iPhone anda. Jika anda membuat sandaran iPhone ke iCloud atau komputer, Apple mungkin memasukkannya dalam sandaran itu. Memadam app akan memadam semuanya. Anda juga boleh mengosongkan lagu terkini dan memadam setlist dari dalam app.

02Apa yang keluar dari iPhone anda, dan siapa yang menerimanya

LyricCam tidak mempunyai pelayan sendiri. Apabila app perlukan sesuatu dari internet, app meminta terus daripada perkhidmatan di bawah. Kami tidak dapat melihat permintaan ini dan tidak menyimpan salinannya.

LRCLIB (lrclib.net), pangkalan data lirik komuniti yang bukan dikendalikan oleh kami Apabila hasil carian dipaparkan, dan apabila anda membuka lagu, memuat turun setlist atau menekan "Lirik salah?". Tajuk, artis, album dan tempoh lagu. Jika carian Apple tidak menemui apa-apa, teks carian anda dihantar ke LRCLIB. App memperkenalkan diri sebagai "LyricCam" dengan nombor versi.

Setiap permintaan internet mendedahkan alamat IP anda dan beberapa butiran teknikal asas, seperti versi app dan versi rangkaian iOS, kepada perkhidmatan yang menerimanya. LyricCam tidak menambah nama anda, akaun, pengecam iklan, pengecam peranti atau lokasi GPS anda pada mana-mana permintaan. Permintaan carian dan lirik dihantar dengan kuki dimatikan.

Apa yang setiap perkhidmatan buat dengan permintaan itu tertakluk kepada dasar masing-masing. Untuk Apple, lihat Dasar Privasi Apple. LRCLIB ialah perkhidmatan komuniti percuma yang bebas. Kami tidak mengawal cara ia mengendalikan atau merekod permintaan.

Tentang mikrofon

Mikrofon digunakan untuk dua perkara sahaja: merakam bunyi video anda, dan mendengar muzik di sekeliling supaya app dapat mengesan lagu dan memastikan lirik selaras. Untuk mengesan lagu, app mendengar kira-kira 12 saat setiap kali. Bunyi yang digunakan untuk mengesan lagu tidak disimpan.

Auto sync dimatikan kecuali anda menghidupkannya. Apabila dihidupkan, app mendengar semula lebih kurang setiap 40 saat, hanya semasa skrin kamera dibuka dengan lagu dimuatkan, dan tidak sekali-kali semasa anda merakam. Apabila anda memainkan pratonton, app cuba memadankan klip pratonton itu sendiri dahulu. Jika gagal dan anda sudah membenarkan mikrofon, app mendengar sehingga 10 saat. iOS memaparkan titik oren setiap kali mikrofon digunakan.

Pautan yang keluar dari app

"Dengar di Apple Music" dan pautan sumber pada halaman konsert dibuka dalam app lain atau pelayar anda. Laman dan app tersebut mempunyai dasar privasi sendiri.
